High-Cost Biologic Medication Request

Prior Authorization

Prior Authorization - Humira (Adalimumab)

Complete prior authorization package for high-cost biologic medication (Humira) for rheumatoid arthritis patient who failed methotrexate, demonstrating thorough clinical justification and evidence-based documentation.

❌ Weak Submission:

"Patient needs Humira for arthritis. Please approve."

Result: Likely denial, requires resubmission, delays care

✅ Strong Submission Package Includes:
  • 1. Completed authorization form with all required fields
  • 2. Clinical narrative with diagnosis (ICD-10: M05.79)
  • 3. Previous treatments tried: Methotrexate (failed after 12 weeks)
  • 4. Supporting lab values showing active disease (elevated CRP, ESR)
  • 5. Evidence-based guidelines cited (ACR recommendations)
  • 6. Physician order and medical records

Result: 90%+ approval rate with this level of preparation

Patient Encounter Documentation

Clinical Documentation

SOAP Note Improvement Sample

Documentation transformation for a 65-year-old male with Type 2 diabetes follow-up, demonstrating comprehensive clinical documentation, proper ICD-10 coding, and appropriate E/M level support.

❌ Before: Incomplete Documentation
Patient: 65yo M
CC: Diabetes follow-up
HPI: Blood sugars high. Not taking meds regularly.
PE: Unremarkable
A/P: Continue metformin. RTC 3 months.
✅ After: Comprehensive SOAP Note
PATIENT: 65-year-old male
CHIEF COMPLAINT: Type 2 diabetes mellitus, uncontrolled

HPI: Blood glucose 200-300 mg/dL past month despite metformin 1000mg BID. Admits non-adherence due to cost/forgetfulness. Denies polyuria, polydipsia, weight changes, or hypoglycemic episodes.

ROS: Reports bilateral foot tingling (new). Denies vision changes, chest pain, SOB.

PE: BP 142/88, HR 78, BMI 32. Diminished monofilament sensation bilaterally.

ASSESSMENT & PLAN:
1. Type 2 DM, uncontrolled (E11.65) - HbA1c ordered, adherence counseling, consider 2nd agent if A1C >8%
2. Diabetic neuropathy (E11.42) - New diagnosis, monitor symptoms
3. HTN, uncontrolled (I10) - Recheck BP in 2 weeks
4. Overdue eye exam - Ophthalmology referral placed

Follow-up: 2 weeks (BP), 1 month (A1C)
Time: 25 minutes (99214)
Documentation Improvements:
  • Complete HPI capturing adherence barriers
  • Identification of previously undocumented complication (neuropathy)
  • Appropriate ICD-10 coding for complications
  • Quality metric compliance (diabetic eye exam referral)
  • Support for appropriate E/M level (99214 vs 99213)
  • Estimated additional revenue per encounter
